## Hospital Emergency Waiting Time ( Divide and Conquer Problems )

A hospital emergency unit keeps a record of how long each patient waited before being seen by a doctor.

At the end of the day, the administration wants to prepare a short service report to identify the two patients who received attention the earliest, so that they can compare those cases with the rest of the day's workflow.

The waiting times in minutes are stored in the following array:

`Waiting_time = [22, 15, 30, 10, 18, 12, 14, 25]`

Design a **Divide and Conquer** algorithm to help the hospital determine which two waiting times should appear first in the report.

Function and variable names should be relevant to the problem.

### Sample Input

```text
8
22 15 30 10 18 12 14 25
 ```

### Sample Output

```text
First two waiting times in the report:
10 12
```
